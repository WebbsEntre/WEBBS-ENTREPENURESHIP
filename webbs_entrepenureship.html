Our Story

More Than a Business —
A Movement

Webbs Entrepreneurship is a sole-proprietorship enterprise built from the ground up by Brian Lee Webb — a visionary entrepreneur who refuses to let geography, resources, or convention limit what's possible. Founded on the belief that bold ideas deserve bold execution, the company operates at the intersection of music, storytelling, and business development.

What started as a personal drive to create has grown into a multi-venture enterprise with a record label, an expanding fictional universe, and a growing network of creative partners. Every arm of the business carries the same DNA: authentic, independent, and built to last.

Webbs Entrepreneurship is headquartered in the American heartland, drawing creative fuel from the raw, real culture of the region — a spirit that runs through everything it produces.

Sole proprietorship with full creative and operational control
Co-creator of the Holler & Hellfire Universe alongside author Angela Smith (pen name Kaylin Renea)
CEO & founder of Holler & Hellfire Records, an independent music label
Committed to amplifying authentic voices in music and storytelling

The Person Behind It

Meet the Founder

Brian Lee Webb

Sole Proprietor & Owner — Webbs Entrepreneurship Co-Creator — Holler & Hellfire Universe CEO — Holler & Hellfire Records

Brian Lee Webb is a 42-year-old entrepreneur, creative builder, and independent operator with an unshakeable belief that authentic ideas — backed by hard work — can become lasting enterprises. He founded Webbs Entrepreneurship as the vehicle for everything he builds: a record label, a fictional universe, and ventures yet to come.

Brian co-created the Holler & Hellfire Universe alongside his fiancée Angela Smith, who authors the book series under the pen name Kaylin Renea. Together they've built a world that reflects the culture, the grit, and the heart of the American heartland.

As CEO of Holler & Hellfire Records, Brian is committed to giving independent artists a real home — a label that operates with integrity, invests in talent, and builds for the long term.
